Razzamatazz Fermented Chilli Sauce

Finishing blend for Rita's fermented Razzamatazz chillies with garlic and lemongrass.

Preparation: 15 minOne small batch, quantity varies

Ingredients

  • 200 g drained fermented Razzamatazz chillies with garlic and lemongrass
  • 75 ml unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 25 ml lime juice
  • 10 g fresh ginger
  • 15–20 g brown sugar
  • 20–40 ml reserved fermentation brine

Method

  1. Use 200 g of an already fermented and drained Razzamatazz chilli, garlic and lemongrass mixture.
  2. Blend with rice vinegar, lime juice, ginger and 15 g brown sugar, adding reserved fermentation brine gradually to reach the desired consistency.
  3. Check flavour and optionally increase the brown sugar towards 20 g.
  4. Remove any tough pieces of lemongrass and pass the sauce through a sieve for a smooth texture.
  5. Measure acidity using a calibrated pH meter. The previously discussed target was pH 3.5–3.8.

This is a finishing recipe for an already fermented base, not a complete fermentation protocol or validated room-temperature preservation process. Refrigerate unless a tested shelf-stable process is used.
