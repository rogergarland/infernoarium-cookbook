SmokeFire Dexter Brisket

The 1.7 kg rolled Dexter brisket cook on the Weber SmokeFire EX6.

Preparation: 20 minCooking: 12 h 30 min1 × 1.7 kg joint

Ingredients

  • 1.7 kg rolled Dexter brisket
  • 2 tbsp coarse black pepper
  • 2 tbsp kosher or flaked salt
  • 1 tsp garlic granules
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Optional: 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Optional: a little Worcestershire sauce or mustard as a binder
  • Weber Competition Blend pellets

Method

  1. Pat the brisket dry. If desired, apply a thin binder, then coat evenly with the pepper, salt, garlic and onion powder rub, adding smoked paprika if using.
  2. Heat the Weber SmokeFire EX6 to 120°C. Place brisket on the grill and cook at 120°C.
  3. When the centre reaches approximately 75°C, wrap the brisket tightly in two layers of butcher’s paper.
  4. Continue at 120°C until approximately 95°C internal temperature and, more importantly, a probe slides in with little resistance.
  5. Rest appropriately before slicing across the grain. The recorded cook took around 12½ hours, but allow the actual probe tenderness to govern the finish.

The rub was originally proposed; the 120°C cook, wrap at 75°C and probe-tender finish at 95°C are from the recorded cook.
