Rhubarb Crumble (700 g Version)

The scaled-up BBC Good Food rhubarb crumble discussed in July 2024.

Ingredients

  • 700 g rhubarb, chopped
  • 140 g golden caster sugar
  • 4.2 tbsp port (optional)
  • 196 g self-raising flour
  • 119 g chilled butter
  • 70 g light brown muscovado sugar
  • 70 g chopped walnuts (optional)

Method

  1. Heat oven to approximately 190°C conventional (170°C fan).
  2. Toss chopped rhubarb with caster sugar and optional port, then place in an ovenproof dish.
  3. Rub chilled butter into flour until crumbly; mix in muscovado sugar and optional walnuts.
  4. Scatter topping over the rhubarb and bake until golden and bubbling, approximately 35–45 minutes.

Ingredient amounts are the recovered 700 g scaling of a BBC Good Food recipe. Oven temperature and baking time are conventional guidance, not directly recovered from the discussion.
