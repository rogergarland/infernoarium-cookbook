Horseradish-Dill Crème Fraîche

Creamy accompaniment developed for the hot-smoked salmon.

Ingredients

  • 300 ml crème fraîche
  • 1–2 tbsp prepared horseradish
  • Fresh dill, chopped to taste
  • Dijon mustard, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Lemon juice, to taste

Method

  1. Mix crème fraîche and horseradish.
  2. Fold in chopped dill, a little Dijon mustard, lemon juice and pepper.
  3. Taste and adjust horseradish and acidity; chill until serving.

Some seasonings were given to taste rather than recorded in fixed quantities.
