Maple-Dijon Hot-Smoked Salmon Side

1.8 kg salmon side with a maple-Dijon-butter glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1.8 kg salmon side
  • 60 g coarse salt
  • 60 g soft brown sugar
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • ½ tsp cider vinegar (later adjustment)

Method

  1. Mix salt, sugar and pepper; cure the salmon side refrigerated for 4 hours.
  2. Rinse and dry, then leave uncovered in the fridge overnight to develop a pellicle.
  3. Smoke with Weber Competition Blend: SmokeBoost 30 minutes, then 110°C.
  4. Mix maple syrup, Dijon, melted butter and cider vinegar. Brush glaze on when fish reaches 46–48°C.
  5. Continue to the original recipe's 54°C internal target; estimated cooking time approximately 1¼–1¾ hours, depending on thickness. Use a thermometer and suitable safety target.

This documents the original 54°C finishing target, not a validated safety standard for every diner. Keep refrigerated.
