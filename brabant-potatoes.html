Brabant Potatoes

Golden New Orleans-style potato cubes with a savoury garlic-butter, herb and Cajun finish.

Serves 4Preparation: 10 minCooking: 20 min

Ingredients

  • 900 g floury potatoes (such as Maris Piper), peeled and cut into 1.3 cm cubes
  • 60 ml neutral vegetable oil
  • 5 g fine salt, or less if the Cajun seasoning contains salt
  • 1 g freshly ground black pepper
  • 5–10 ml Cajun or Creole seasoning, to taste
  • 50 g spring onions, finely sliced
  • 15 g fresh parsley, chopped
  • 30 ml Worcestershire sauce
  • 18 g fresh garlic, finely chopped
  • 55 g unsalted butter

Method

  1. Place the potato cubes in cold water in a saucepan. Bring to the boil and simmer gently for 4–5 minutes; they should just begin to soften, not fall apart. Drain thoroughly and leave to steam-dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide frying pan over medium-high heat. Spread out the cubes in one layer, season lightly with salt and black pepper, and fry for 10–12 minutes, turning or shaking regularly, until the surfaces are crisp and golden. Fry in batches if the pan is too small.
  3. Scatter in the spring onions, parsley, garlic and Cajun seasoning. Add the Worcestershire sauce and toss continuously for about 1 minute so the garlic becomes fragrant without catching.
  4. Add the butter, toss until melted and glossy, then serve immediately. Taste before adding more salt.
Kitchen notes: Maris Piper is a good UK substitute for American russet potatoes. Dry the cubes thoroughly and leave room in the pan. If using a salted Cajun mix, hold back some of the added salt. Metric quantities are rounded approximations of the original US measures.
