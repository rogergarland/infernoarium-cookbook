Ninja Steam-Roasted Chicken with Root Vegetables

Trial recipe for a 1.8 kg probe-controlled steam-roasted chicken, soft potatoes, carrots and parsnips, with 1,000 W microwave-steamed greens.

1.8 kg chicken4–5 servingsSteam Roast: 180°CProbe target: 75°CRest: 10–15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 × 1.8 kg whole chicken
  • 600 g Maris Piper potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks
  • 3 carrots, cut into thick batons
  • 3 parsnips, quartered lengthways
  • 200 ml chicken stock (check the appliance's minimum liquid requirement)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Rosemary, thyme and garlic, to taste
  • Optional: 25 g softened butter for chicken skin
  • Broccoli florets and Brussels sprouts, quantities to suit

Method

  1. Toss the potatoes, carrots and parsnips with the olive oil, salt and pepper. Add 200 ml stock to the Ninja Foodi MAX 15-in-1 cooking pot and arrange the root vegetables in the bottom.
  2. Set the reversible rack above the vegetables. Pat the chicken dry, season with herbs, garlic and optional butter, then place breast-side up on the rack. Keep the chicken clear of the lid.
  3. Insert the Ninja temperature probe into the thickest part of the chicken breast without touching bone. Select Combi-Steam / Steam Roast, 180°C and a probe target of 75°C, following the appliance prompts. The appliance controls the cooking time; 45–55 minutes was an initial estimate, not a guaranteed duration.
  4. When the Ninja indicates completion, verify at least 75°C in the thickest parts of both breast and thigh using a food thermometer; continue cooking if necessary. Also confirm the vegetables are tender and cook them longer if needed.
  5. Rest chicken 10–15 minutes before carving. The potatoes will be soft and absorb the stock and chicken juices, not crispy.
  6. For the greens, use a microwave steamer in a 1,000 W microwave with a little water. Start Brussels sprouts at 3–5 minutes and broccoli florets at 2–3 minutes; check for tenderness and cook in 30-second increments as required. If steaming together, start sprouts for 2 minutes, then add broccoli for a further 2–3 minutes. Take care with escaping steam.
  7. Steam the greens during the chicken resting period and serve everything together.

Test version: Published before the first cook. Probe-controlled cooking time and how tender the vegetables become still need confirming. Follow the appliance's own minimum-liquid and rack-position instructions. Microwave timings depend on vegetable size, quantity and steamer design. Beware of hot steam.
