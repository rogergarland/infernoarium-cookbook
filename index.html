Rich Slow-Cooked
Dexter Beef Stew

Deeply savoury Dexter beef, slow-cooked with red wine, chestnut mushrooms, thyme and Dijon mustard.

Prep: approx. 45 minLOW: 9–10 hoursHIGH: 5–6 hoursYield: approx. 8–10 portions

Ingredients

  • 2.3 kg diced Dexter beef
  • 3 onions, chopped
  • 8 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 5 celery sticks, chopped
  • 5–6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200–250 g chestnut mushrooms
  • 300 ml dry red wine
  • 900 ml boiling water
  • 2 Knorr Rich Beef Stock Pots
  • 5 tbsp tomato purée
  • 5 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 5 tbsp oil, for browning
  • Fresh thyme, to taste
  • 5–6 bay leaves
  • Fresh parsley, to finish
  • 25–30 g cold unsalted butter
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Cornflour, optional, for thickening

Method

  1. Pat the beef dry. Brown it in batches in hot oil, then transfer to the slow cooker.
  2. Soften the onions, carrots and celery in the pan. Add the garlic for 30–60 seconds. Brown the mushrooms separately.
  3. Stir in the tomato purée. Pour in the red wine, scrape up the browned bits, and simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Transfer everything to the slow cooker. Add the stock pots dissolved in 900 ml boiling water, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, thyme, bay leaves and black pepper.
  5. Cook on LOW for 9–10 hours, or HIGH for 5–6 hours, until the beef is tender.
  6. Remove bay leaves. If necessary, thicken with a cornflour slurry. Stir in cold butter and finish with parsley. Taste before adding salt.
Kitchen note: Hold back additional salt until the end: the stock pots and Worcestershire sauce are already seasoned. If your slow cooker is very full, check its maximum fill line before starting.
