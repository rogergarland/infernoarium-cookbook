Scotch Bonnet & Ginger Jam

A bold sweet-hot chilli jam made with Scotch bonnets, peppers, ginger and two vinegars.

Preparation: 30 minCooking: 1 h 10 minSeveral jars; yield varies

Ingredients

  • 277 g Scotch Bonnet chillies
  • 1 kg red peppers
  • 100 g peeled fresh ginger
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 kg jam sugar
  • 500 ml cider vinegar
  • 500 ml red-wine vinegar
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Prepare and roughly chop the Scotch bonnets, red peppers, ginger and garlic. Wear gloves when handling the chillies.
  2. Blitz the chillies, peppers, ginger and garlic to a finely chopped mixture.
  3. Combine with jam sugar, cider vinegar, red-wine vinegar and salt in a large preserving pan. Heat gently, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Boil uncovered for approximately 45–70 minutes, stirring as needed, until thick and glossy. Aim for 104–105°C and confirm with a cold-plate wrinkle test.
  5. Let stand for 5–10 minutes, then carefully transfer to clean, hot sterilised jars and close.

Keep refrigerated. Sterilised jars alone do not establish a validated shelf-stable canning process.
