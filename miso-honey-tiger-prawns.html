Miso-Soy-Honey Tiger Prawns

The 1 kg batch with garlic, ginger and sesame-lemon butter.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg tiger prawns
  • 8 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3.5 cm fresh ginger, grated
  • 2⅔ tbsp miso paste
  • 1⅓ tbsp soy sauce
  • 2⅔ tbsp honey
  • 105 g butter
  • 1⅓ tsp sesame oil
  • Zest and juice of approximately 1⅓ lemons
  • Tahini, sesame seeds and sliced spring onions, to taste

Method

  1. Mix miso, soy, honey, garlic and grated ginger. Coat prepared prawns in the mixture; keep chilled until ready to cook.
  2. Melt butter with sesame oil and lemon zest and juice, stirring to combine.
  3. Cook prawns over a hot grill or in a pan, turning until opaque, firm and fully cooked.
  4. Drizzle with sesame-lemon butter and finish with tahini, sesame seeds and spring onions as desired.

The 1 kg quantities are recovered; exact searing times were not captured and depend on prawn size.
