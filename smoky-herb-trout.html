Smoky Herb Trout Fillets

Four trout fillets with butter, herbs, Dijon and honey.

Ingredients

  • 4 trout fillets
  • 50 g butter
  • Garlic, parsley, dill and thyme to taste
  • Dijon mustard, to taste
  • Honey, to taste

Method

  1. Melt butter and combine with garlic, parsley, dill, thyme, mustard and honey.
  2. Brush trout fillets with herb butter.
  3. Cook on SmokeFire at 180°C for approximately 10–15 minutes; optional 120–130°C smoke start was also discussed.
  4. Check the thickest part with a thermometer. The original proposal mentioned 52–55°C; use a target suitable for food safety and diners.

Proposed recipe; seasoning amounts were not fixed and the original temperature was a culinary preference, not a universal safety endpoint.
