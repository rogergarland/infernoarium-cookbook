Roasted Jalapeño Hummus

A creamy chickpea hummus with roasted jalapeños, tahini and lemon.

Preparation: 10 minCooking: 20 minOne medium bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas (approximately 240 g drained)
  • 3–4 jalapeños
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt, adjust to taste
  • 2–4 tbsp ice-cold water

Method

  1. Heat oven to 220°C conventional (200°C fan). Roast jalapeños for 15–20 minutes, turning once, until softened and lightly charred.
  2. Let the jalapeños cool, remove stalks and roughly chop. Remove seeds if less heat is wanted.
  3. Drain chickpeas. Blend chickpeas, jalapeños, tahini, garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, cumin, smoked paprika and salt for 2–3 minutes until smooth.
  4. Add 2–4 tablespoons of ice-cold water a little at a time and blend until creamy. Taste and adjust lemon and salt.

Jalapeño heat varies considerably. Refrigerate promptly; this is a fresh dip, not a shelf-stable preserve.
