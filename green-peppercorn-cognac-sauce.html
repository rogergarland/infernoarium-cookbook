Green Peppercorn & Cognac Sauce

About 200 ml of sauce for two, served with steak.

Ingredients

  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp brined green peppercorns
  • 40 ml cognac
  • 100 ml beef stock
  • 120 ml double cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)

Method

  1. Soften shallot gently in butter; add green peppercorns and warm briefly.
  2. Add cognac and simmer until reduced by approximately half.
  3. Add stock and reduce by approximately half.
  4. Stir in cream, Dijon and optional Worcestershire sauce, and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon.
