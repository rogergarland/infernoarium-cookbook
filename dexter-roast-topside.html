Roast Dexter Topside

A 1.1 kg well-aged Dexter topside joint, cooked to a rosy centre.

Preparation: 10 minCooking: 55 min1 × 1.1 kg joint

Ingredients

  • 1.1 kg grass-fed, well-aged Dexter topside beef
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • A little cooking oil, optional

Method

  1. Remove packaging and pat the joint dry. Season the outside to taste.
  2. Preheat oven to 220°C conventional or 200°C fan.
  3. Roast for 20 minutes, then lower to 160°C conventional or 140°C fan.
  4. Continue until the centre reads 52–53°C for the originally intended finish; use a probe thermometer rather than time alone.
  5. Rest loosely covered with foil for approximately 30 minutes before thinly slicing across the grain.

Approximately 50–55 minutes total oven time was the original estimate. Internal temperature can rise during resting. This is a medium-rare approach; vulnerable diners should follow appropriate food-safety guidance.
