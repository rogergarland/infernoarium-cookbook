Hereford Caveman Steak

Proposed cook for a 2.9 kg Hereford caveman steak using gas BBQ and SmokeFire.

Ingredients

  • 2.9 kg Hereford caveman steak
  • Approximately 29 g salt, for dry brining
  • 1½ tbsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp garlic granules
  • 1 tbsp onion granules

Method

  1. Salt the steak and dry-brine uncovered in the fridge overnight.
  2. Add pepper, garlic granules and onion granules.
  3. Sear on a very hot gas BBQ for approximately 3–5 minutes per side.
  4. Move to SmokeFire at 120–130°C until 53–54°C internal for the discussed medium-rare target, or 56–58°C for medium.
  5. Rest for 30–45 minutes and thinly slice across the grain.

This was a proposed method. The optional wrapping step was discussed but was not part of the standard method.
