Beef-Dripping Roast Potatoes

Crisp roast potatoes finished with garlic and rosemary.

Preparation: 20 minCooking: 1 h 15 minScale to the number of diners

Ingredients

  • Potatoes, quantity to suit
  • Beef dripping, enough to coat the roasting tray and potatoes
  • Salt, for parboiling and seasoning
  • Garlic, to taste
  • Fresh rosemary, to taste

Method

  1. Peel or prepare potatoes and cut into generously sized, even pieces.
  2. Parboil in salted water for 8–10 minutes. Drain, rough up the edges and steam-dry for around 5 minutes.
  3. Heat the oven to 220°C conventional or 200°C fan with beef dripping in the roasting tray.
  4. Add potatoes carefully and roast for 60–75 minutes, turning every 20–25 minutes.
  5. Add garlic and rosemary during the final 30–40 minutes. Finish when deeply golden and crisp.

The original discussion recorded the method and temperatures but not specific weights for potatoes, garlic or dripping.
