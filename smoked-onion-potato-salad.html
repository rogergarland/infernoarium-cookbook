Smoked Onion & Herb Potato Salad

Smoked sweet onion with potatoes, a creamy dressing and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg baby potatoes
  • 1 large sweet or Spanish onion
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp Greek yoghurt or sour cream
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • Parsley, chives or dill to taste
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Halve onion through the root, oil and season. Smoke at 110–120°C for approximately 2–3 hours until tender.
  2. Boil potatoes until just tender, drain and cool slightly.
  3. Mix mayonnaise, yoghurt, Dijon, vinegar and remaining oil into a dressing.
  4. Chop smoked onion and toss through potatoes with the dressing and herbs. Season to taste.

A proposed BBQ side from August 2025; adjust dressing to taste.
