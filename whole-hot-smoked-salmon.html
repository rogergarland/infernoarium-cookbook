Whole Hot-Smoked Salmon (2.2 kg)

Whole gutted, head-on salmon on the SmokeFire EX6.

Ingredients

  • 2.2 kg whole gutted salmon, head on
  • 75 g coarse salt
  • 75 g light soft brown sugar
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Lemon and fresh dill for cavity (optional)
  • Maple syrup, 2 tbsp (optional glaze)

Method

  1. Mix salt, brown sugar and pepper. Cover salmon evenly; cure refrigerated for 2–4 hours (2 hours was used in a later version).
  2. Rinse cure lightly, pat dry and let salmon air-dry uncovered in the fridge 1–3 hours to form a pellicle.
  3. Fill cavity with lemon and dill, if using. Place directly on SmokeFire rather than a plank.
  4. Run SmokeBoost approximately 30 minutes, then set grill to 110°C and cook to the chosen internal temperature, probing thickest section.
  5. For the original June formulation, target 54°C and rest 10–15 minutes. A later June discussion recommended 60–63°C, a more conservative target. If using maple, brush on near 50°C.

Recorded target temperatures differ between the original and follow-up discussions. Use a food thermometer and choose an appropriate food-safety target for your diners. This is hot smoked, not shelf-stable.
